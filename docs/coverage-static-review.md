## Evidence labels and limits

- **DIRECT-RUNTIME-CANDIDATE**: test source calls real product paths against scratch PDO/SQL and contains state/result assertions. A candidate label alone is not run evidence; any observed run status is stated separately and tied to its evidence file/version.
- **COMPONENT-CANDIDATE**: a real component method is exercised with a fake LDAP/native boundary, or a narrower same-process component check. It does not prove the external protocol or full caller schedule.
- **STATIC-ONLY**: assertions inspect source/route shape, simulated DB responses, or fixture inputs rather than the specified real writer schedule.
- **NOT_COVERED**: no current test assertion was found that demonstrates the specified case.
- **HISTORICAL / SEPARATE**: prior F1/P1/Go results may support their original contract only. They are not upgraded to B-case evidence here. P2 files not known to have completed the current regression remain unexecuted candidates, regardless of their names, output strings, or source comments.

These targeted results do not establish the full acceptance matrix. The session fixture's later `fetchAll`/stderr/SQL-shape edits are not covered by those targeted results.
